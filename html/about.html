Who we are

About the National Highway Authority

The National Highway Authority is responsible for Pakistan's national highway and motorway network.

Our work spans the full road lifecycle: planning and developing routes, and operating and maintaining them. Reliable road connections help people travel, link regions and support the movement of goods throughout the country.

Through our projects and services, NHA works toward a modern, safe and efficient highway network for a progressive Pakistan.

Our purpose

To connect people and places with road infrastructure that serves Pakistan's communities and economy.
