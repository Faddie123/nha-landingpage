Road Safety

Simple road-safety reminders for safer journeys on Pakistan's highways and motorways.

General travel guidance is provided below. For emergencies or live road conditions, follow official instructions and contact the relevant authorities.

A safer journey starts with you

Five reminders for every road

Select a reminder to see how a small action can help protect everyone on the road.

Protect every passenger

Fasten your seat belt

Wear your seat belt on every trip, even for short journeys. Make sure passengers are buckled up and children are seated safely before you set off.

Keep your attention on the road

No mobile while driving

Calls, messages, and screen checks take your attention away from traffic. Set your route before you leave, and pull over somewhere safe before using your phone.

Arrive safely

Speed thrills, but kills

Follow posted speed limits and slow down when visibility, weather, traffic, or the road surface makes driving more difficult. Leave yourself time to react.

Share the road

Give pedestrians the right to walk

Watch for people crossing, especially near junctions and pedestrian crossings. Slow down, give them room, and stop when it is safe for them to cross.

Leave room to react

Don't tailgate

Keep a safe following distance so you have time to respond if the vehicle ahead slows suddenly. Increase the gap in poor weather or when visibility is reduced.
