Road Safety

Simple road-safety reminders for safer journeys on Pakistan's highways and motorways.

General travel guidance is provided below. For emergencies or live road conditions, follow official instructions and contact the relevant authorities.

Keep a safe speed

Observe posted speed limits and adjust your speed for traffic, visibility, and road conditions.

Keep a safe distance

Leave enough space between vehicles and avoid sudden lane changes or unsafe overtaking.

Use seat belts

Wear a seat belt on every trip and make sure passengers are secured before moving.

Avoid distractions

Do not use a handheld phone while driving. Pull over safely before making a call or checking a device.

Rest before driving

Do not drive when tired. Take breaks on long journeys and stop in a safe place if you feel drowsy.

Check your vehicle

Before departure, check tyres, brakes, lights and fuel, and carry required safety equipment.
