Harassment at Workplace

For a workplace harassment complaint, follow the applicable NHA policy and submit your concern through the designated inquiry committee or official channel listed in the relevant policy.

This page is informational and does not receive complaints. To protect confidentiality, share details only through an authorized official channel.

Before you contact the designated office

  • Refer to the applicable workplace harassment policy and its reporting instructions.
  • Record dates and relevant details you may need to provide.
  • Use the contact details and procedure in the official NHA policy or notice.
